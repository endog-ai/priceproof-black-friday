How these deals were checked

Each price on this page was read on 27 Nov 2026 at the store named on the deal, or in a deals source that names the store — Popular Science, 9to5Toys, 9to5Google, Tom’s Guide, Slickdeals, 2nd Shift, FullCleared, WalletHub, Koupon and the Black Friday diary. The source and its date are printed on every deal, and the link opens it. Discounts are computed from the sale price and the usual price the same source showed, so the maths always adds up.

Every deal carries an honest verdict: New low means the lowest price ever recorded for that product; Strong deal is a deep cut on a well-known product; Solid deal is a genuine saving; Routine sale is a small cut — fine if you need it now, not a bargain to chase. Where the same product is cheaper at another store, the card says so.
